01 / MOTIVATION & METHODFROM DECISIONS TO EVIDENCE

From a source label to visible evidence.

A correct real or AI-generated prediction does not guarantee that a detector has recovered the visible artifacts. It may overlook a relevant event, misinterpret what it sees, or reach the right verdict with incomplete evidence.

Verus-Insight separates this process into three complementary capabilities, and learns them through human-anchored full-process supervision.

  1. 01

    Inspection Planning

    Determine what to inspect with content-specific criteria.

  2. 02

    Evidence Verification

    Verify each criterion against the video and recover concrete artifact evidence.

  3. 03

    Evidence Aggregation

    Integrate the verified observations into the final source decision.

Human-anchored, full-process supervision.

Human artifact annotations become outcome-neutral inspection criteria paired with their verification targets. Independently verified normal checks complement the artifact examples. The complete supervision follows Context → Rubric → Audit → Aggregation.

Built on Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct, Verus-Insight learns through tracking and rubric warm-up, full-process supervised fine-tuning, and GRPO. At inference, the model generates its own inspection criteria and verifies them against the video.

Inspection planning and artifact finding

P / R / F1 are precision, recall, and F1 (%; ↑ higher is better). Plan is evaluated on artifact-positive videos; Finding on the full set. Both use one-to-one semantic matching. Ranking marks follow the manuscript's open-source comparison.

On RewardData, Verus-Insight increases Finding F1 from 3.38 for its backbone to 35.42, with gains in both precision and recall. An unmatched reference comparison does not, by itself, establish that a visual observation is unsupported.

What each training component contributes

BA: balanced accuracy. All values are percentages (↑ higher is better). Best and second-best markings follow the manuscript across the listed variants.

The full model has the highest Plan F1 and Finding F1 on both benchmarks. Removing the Stage-II rubric slightly improves BA, while reducing both fine-grained metrics. Stronger source classification does not necessarily imply better artifact understanding.

How to read these results

Balanced accuracy averages recall over the real and AI-generated classes. Plan measures coverage of inspection targets associated with reference artifacts. Finding measures recovery of the artifacts themselves. Predictions are matched one-to-one against the reference annotations; unmatched predicted findings count as false positives in this scoring protocol.

Results are transcribed from the manuscript and have not been recomputed for this release. Reference matching and visual validity are different questions. ViF-Bench source classification and the ViF-CoT-4K finding subset are separate evaluations.
